# Twinwick

A candle-heap puzzle for iPhone. Coming soon to the App Store.

The market is over and the candles are in a heap. Stand them up, three alike, and watch them burn away.

120 free levels · No timers · No lives · No ads

## How it plays: count the holders before you pull a wick

1. **Tap any candle you can see.** Even one half buried in the heap. It stands up in one of seven brass holders on the wall rail.
2. **Three alike burn away.** They catch for a moment, go out in a curl of smoke, and free their holders.
3. **Tied candles travel together.** Two or three candles on one wick go up as a group, buried ones included. If the group will not fit, the wick goes taut and nothing is lost, so count the free holders first.
4. **Warm wax waits.** A warm candle carries a number and lifts only after that many triples (sets of three alike) have burned.
5. **Clear the cloth to shelve the batch**, which wins the level. Seven full holders with no three alike ends the try; play it again for free.

## What is in the box: 120 levels, three tools, nothing chasing you

Chapters: 01 Market Stall · 02 Dipping Room · 03 Long Wicks · 04 Twin Shades · 05 Still Warm · 06 Festival Order

- **Trim** cuts a wick, so its candles go alone.
- **Put Back** returns your last move to the heap.
- **Spare Holder** adds an eighth holder for the rest of the level.

Each level earns one to three flames: use five holders or fewer for all three. Tools cost wax, which you earn by winning. Optional wax packs are sold in the app; every level stays free.

Twinwick for iPhone. No account, no tracking: your progress stays on your device.
