# Twinwick Privacy Policy

Twinwick · last updated 19 November 2026

Twinwick is a puzzle game for iPhone. It does not ask who you are, it collects no personal data, and nothing about you leaves your device.

## What stays on your device

- Your progress: the levels you have cleared and the flames you earned on each.
- Your wax balance and how many of each tool (Trim, Put Back, Spare Holder) you hold.
- Your settings: whether sound is on.
- The transaction ids of wax packs already credited, so the same purchase is never counted twice.

All of this is kept in the app's own storage on your device (iOS app storage, through SharedPreferences). It is not sent anywhere, not synced between devices, and deleting the app removes it.

## What we do not do

- No account or sign-in.
- No advertising and no ad networks.
- No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
- No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

## Purchases

All 120 levels are free. Optional wax packs are sold through Apple's App Store (StoreKit). Apple processes the payment under its own privacy policy; we never see your name, Apple ID or payment details. The app only receives a transaction id from the App Store so it can add the wax to your balance. Wax packs are consumable, so *Settings → Restore purchases* has nothing to bring back.

## Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
